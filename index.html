China 🇨🇳

A Country of History, Culture and Innovation

History Culture Places Facts

History

China has one of the oldest continuous civilizations in the world. Its history spans thousands of years and includes many dynasties, important inventions, wars and cultural changes.

Some important inventions associated with ancient China include paper, printing, gunpowder and the compass.

Chinese Culture

Food

Chinese cuisine is very diverse. Popular dishes include dumplings, noodles, fried rice and Peking duck.

Language

Mandarin Chinese is the most widely spoken language in China. Chinese writing uses characters instead of an alphabet.

Traditions

Chinese traditions include festivals such as the Lunar New Year, the Lantern Festival and the Dragon Boat Festival.

Famous Places

The Great Wall

The Great Wall is one of China's most famous landmarks. It was built and expanded over different periods of Chinese history.

The Forbidden City

Located in Beijing, the Forbidden City was the imperial palace of China's emperors for centuries.

Shanghai

Shanghai is one of China's largest cities and is known for its modern skyline, technology and international importance.

Interesting Facts

  • China is located in East Asia.
  • The capital city is Beijing.
  • The official currency is the renminbi (yuan).
  • The giant panda is native to China.
  • The Great Wall is one of China's most famous landmarks.
  • China has a very diverse geography, including mountains, deserts and plains.
